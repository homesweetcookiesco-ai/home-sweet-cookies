Our Story

Warm, familiar, unforgettable.

Home Sweet Cookies was created around a simple idea: the best desserts feel familiar. Every cookie is designed to be generous, comforting, and memorable — the kind of treat you bring home to share, gift to someone you love, or keep all to yourself.

Our soft blue palette, classic flavors, and milk-bar pairings bring an elevated, nostalgic bakery experience to every order.
